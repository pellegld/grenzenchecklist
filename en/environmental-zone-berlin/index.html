Environmental zone Berlin: Umweltzone Berlin

The detailed rule text on this page is only available in Dutch — the labels above are translated so you can find and share the right page.

Can I drive to Berlin with my car?

Groene Umweltplakette vereist: diesel vanaf Euro 4 (of Euro 3 met roetfilter), benzine vanaf Euro 1 met geregelde katalysator.

What emission standard do I need in Berlin?

diesel from Euro 4, petrol from Euro 1.

Details

De zone ligt binnen de S-Bahn-ring.
