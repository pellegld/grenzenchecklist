Environmental zones in Belgium: can my car enter?

The detailed rule text on this page is only available in Dutch — the labels above are translated so you can find and share the right page.

Can my car enter the environmental zone in Belgium?

diesel from Euro 6, petrol from Euro 3 (Brussel, de strengste zone).

What do I need to arrange for the environmental zone in Belgium?

Registreer je buitenlandse kenteken vooraf en gratis, per stad apart: Brussel via lez.brussels (5 jaar geldig), Antwerpen via slimnaarantwerpen.be, Gent via stad.gent. Er is geen fysieke sticker.
