Toll in Croatia: rates and toll points

The detailed rule text on this page is only available in Dutch — the labels above are translated so you can find and share the right page.

Is there toll in Croatia?

Yes, approx. €0.08 per km on toll roads.

Distance-based toll

Approx. €0.08 per kilometer on toll roads. De autocesta is tolweg met tolpoortjes; in het hoogseizoen lange wachttijden richting de kust.
