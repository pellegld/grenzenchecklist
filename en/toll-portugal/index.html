Toll in Portugal: rates and toll points

The detailed rule text on this page is only available in Dutch — the labels above are translated so you can find and share the right page.

Is there toll in Portugal?

Yes, approx. €0.09 per km on toll roads.

Distance-based toll

Approx. €0.09 per kilometer on toll roads. Op de ex-SCUT-snelwegen bestaan geen tolhuisjes; registreer je kenteken vooraf via EASYToll of portugaltolls.com.
